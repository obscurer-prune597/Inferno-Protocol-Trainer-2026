## 🎮 About the Game

Inferno Protocol is a first-person co-op survival horror game developed and published by Tektonic Interactive. It entered Early Access on August 28, 2026, for PC. Players explore a vast, constantly changing labyrinth alone or with up to six players, scavenge materials, craft equipment, fight creatures, and search for a way out.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Inferno Protocol**. This trainer provides configurable assistance for exploration, survival, crafting, inventory management, and combat. It is designed for the game's maze navigation, resource gathering, firearms, traps, creature harvesting, and syringe-based ability systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed solo experience, faster crafting and exploration, easier testing of weapons and abilities, or assistance learning the labyrinth and survival systems.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer's movement options to maintain stamina during long expeditions and maze traversal. Syringe-based abilities can change movement by granting effects such as dash or double jump, while normal sprinting and vertical traversal remain part of the game's first-person exploration.

### Tools & Inventory

Gather scrap metal, wires, creature parts, and other materials throughout the labyrinth. Harvested bones, armor, flesh, and organs can support crafting. Resource and crafting options are most useful when preparing weapons, gear, traps, and supplies at the central settlement or other safe areas.

### World & Environment

The labyrinth is circular, massive, and constantly changing, with zones representing different environments. Navigation depends on memory and environmental markers such as spray paint, chalk, dropped objects, notebooks, and paper maps. The trainer can reduce survival pressure while you learn routes, inspect settlements, trade with NPCs, and search for notes, loaders, and exits.

### Combat & Weapons

Inferno Protocol uses first-person combat against creatures that can react to sound and scent. Use firearms, experimental weapons, traps, and harvested resources to survive encounters. Unlimited ammunition, no reload, increased damage, and health protection can be enabled individually so players can practice combat without removing every survival challenge.
